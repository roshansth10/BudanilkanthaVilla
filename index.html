01 / Philosophy
Light · Material · Stillness

Conceived as a sanctuary folded into the hillside.

Morning light breaks across the Ganesh Himal before touching the timber rafters of the upper terrace. Built with slate, oiled teak, and hand-chiseled local stone, the residence was conceived not as an imposing monument, but as a quiet refuge where the valley mist rises to meet the forest edge.
