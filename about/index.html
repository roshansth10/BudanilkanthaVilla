Materials

Honest stone and enduring joinery.

The foundation and lower retaining walls are built from dense Godawari blue stone, hand-dressed on site to anchor the volume into the bedrock. Deep overhangs protect the interior from monsoon rain while welcoming the low winter sun deep into the formal living spaces.

Every door frame, window reveal, and floating staircase step is milled from seasoned Burma teak, left untreated with chemical lacquers to allow the wood grain to breathe and mature with age.

Spacial Flow

Continuous circulation between terrace and fire.

The two primary living salons are arranged across split levels to create distinct zones of intimacy without erecting solid dividing barriers. A central double-sided stone hearth warms the dining room in November while sliding glass walls disappear into hidden wall pockets when the spring warmth returns.

The open culinary kitchen is equipped with custom walnut cabinetry, solid quartzite countertops, and a secondary butler pantry that allows formal entertaining without kitchen noise in the salon.

Sanctuary

Baths open to the morning ridge.

Five bedroom suites are positioned along the upper level, each with private balconies framing unobstructed views of the forested foothills. The master bathroom features a monolithic soaking tub set directly against full-height glazing, offering absolute seclusion above the tree line.
